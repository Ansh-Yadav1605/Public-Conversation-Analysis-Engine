# How to Read This Analysis Report
### A Guide for Product Managers, Designers, and Stakeholders

---

## 1. What is this Report?

This report is the output of the **Public Conversation Analysis Engine**. It analyzes thousands of real, unsolicited public conversations across multiple channels (App Store reviews, Google Play reviews, Reddit discussions, YouTube comments, Fashion Forums, and Social media) to diagnose **why fashion e-commerce users wishlist items but never purchase them**.

## 3. How to Interpret the Scoring System

Every Opportunity Area is evaluated across three objective dimensions normalized from 0.0 to 1.0:

| Metric | Meaning | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Frequency Score** | **Signal Volume** | How often this friction point is mentioned relative to other issues in the dataset. |
| **Severity Score** | **Pain Intensity** | How intensely users describe the barrier (e.g. "completely wrong", "impossible", "hate" vs. "minor issue"). |
| **Evidence Strength** | **Source Diversity × Confidence** | How broadly the pattern is confirmed across independent platforms (prevents platform-specific bias). |
| **Composite Priority Score** | **Overall Impact Priority** | Weighted combination (`0.40 × Frequency + 0.35 × Severity + 0.25 × Evidence Strength`). |

---

## 4. Cross-Source Validation Guarantee

Every Opportunity Area included in this report has passed a strict **Cross-Source Evidence Filter**:
- A candidate opportunity **must appear in at least 2 independent source types** (e.g. Reddit + Play Store).
- Isolated single-platform rants or platform-specific UI glitches are automatically filtered out.

---

## 5. How to Use this Report in Product Planning

1. **Immediate Discovery Prioritization:** Focus initial design sprints and product hypotheses on **Rank #1 to #5 Opportunity Areas**.
2. **User Interview Recruitment Screener:** Use the **Target User Segments** listed in Section 3 to recruit participants for primary qualitative interviews.
3. **Hypothesis Formulation:** Use the **Opportunity Statements** directly as problem hypotheses for A/B testing and prototype evaluation.
4. **Behavioral Question Answers:** Reference Section 2 when building the product strategy deck or answering executive questions on specific friction points.
